# ASD-STE100 for technical details

Use this summary for routine technical writing. Do not download or load the full PDF by default. Consult [ASD-STE100 Issue 9][standard] only for a specific rule or vocabulary question this summary cannot answer, or an explicit compliance request. Retrieve the relevant pages or entries instead of reading the whole standard. If they are unavailable, disclose the limitation.

This selective summary does not establish compliance with the full standard or its controlled dictionary.

| Topic | Practical application | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terminology | Keep the name of a technical item consistent. | 1.11 |
| Voice | Identify the actor and action. Descriptive text permits passive wording when the actor is unknown. | 3.6 |
| Clarity | Keep complete, unambiguous sentences; retain words needed to identify the subject and action. | 4.1–4.2 |
| Procedures | Limit sentences to 20 words; normally give one instruction per sentence, in command form. Put a prerequisite condition before its action. | 5.1–5.4 |
| Descriptions | Introduce information gradually, connect related ideas, and limit sentences to 25 words. Keep one topic per paragraph. | 6.1–6.5 |

The overview-to-detail order and reference-style citations are First Skills requirements. Formal STE compliance also needs the standard's vocabulary and applicable rules checked.
